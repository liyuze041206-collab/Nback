# 第二版深浅主题字母生产素材

生成方式：内置 image_gen；每个字母和主题单独一次调用，共八张透明 PNG。引用已展示的对应主题预览板，把大号徽章转换成独立素材。保留原 PNG alpha，网页通过主题切换两套资源。文件名为 `letter-{A|B|C|D}-{dark|light}-v2.png`，位于本目录。

逐张核对字母拼写，八张均为 1254×1254 RGBA，alpha 范围 0–255。网页用 CSS 控制外部透明留白及缩小显示，不修改图像像素。

## 完整提示词

### dark A

Reference: docs/brand/letter-redesign-v2/dark-preview.png

Use case: background-extraction / precise-object-edit. The input is the approved GSPM-Net A/B/C/D design preview board. Extract ONLY the LARGE TOP ROW badge for the exact capital letter "A". Preserve that badge's exact letterform, weight, rounded-square border, material, colors and small gold accent from the reference. Remove all other letters, samples and the whole preview-board backdrop. Deliver one standalone front-facing badge centered on a genuinely transparent square PNG canvas, badge occupies 90% of the width, equal clean transparent padding on all sides. Inside the rounded square keep its designed surface; outside it and around the corners must be fully transparent. No redesign, no added labels, no new bevels, no rotations, no extra objects. Keep the glyph perfectly upright and optically centered, readable when the full badge is 32px square. Variant: dark; the smoky dark navy badge with ice-blue glyph and thin blue border, small gold tick.
